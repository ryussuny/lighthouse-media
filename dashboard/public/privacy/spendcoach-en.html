3. What is sent to the server when you turn on AI

Automatic categorizing and the weekly coach work only after you tap "Agree and turn on" on the "Turn on AI features" screen. If you don't agree, entries, budgets and the subscription check still work and you pick categories yourself. You can turn the AI feature off at any time in AI settings.

The server does not store the text you send or the AI's answer. It handles the request and returns the answer. Server records keep only processing details such as the account identifier, feature name, amount used, cost and status — not your text.

6. Why we use information

Recording, categorizing and showing spending; comparing with your monthly budget; finding repeating payments (subscriptions) and computing renewal dates; renewal reminders; and, if you turn on AI, suggesting categories and producing weekly coach reports; running the server, managing usage and preventing misuse. We do not use it for other purposes.

7. Keeping and deleting; how to ask for deletion

Information on your device

It stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete entries and subscriptions one by one in the app (tap the entry or subscription, then "Delete"). To erase everything at once, clear this app's storage (data) in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Deleting an entry keeps the merchant rule learned from it; clearing app data removes the rules too.

Information on the server (if you turned on AI)

The server has an account-deletion function. When it runs, the account document, the usage records under it and the duplicate-request records are deleted; the account identifier stays in a deletion record and a re-registration guard for 30 days and is then deleted automatically. Anonymous app-wide totals remain.

This version of the app does not yet have an in-app "Delete account" screen. To have your server information deleted, please email ryussuny@gmail.com. If you include roughly when you first used the app and whether you used the AI feature, we will delete what we can identify within 10 days and tell you the result. Because the app does not show you an account identifier, we will tell you if we cannot identify your account. Uninstalling the app deletes all records stored on this phone.

Information that the law requires us to keep, or that is needed for security and fraud prevention, may be kept as long as needed; this policy will state what and for how long.

8. Your rights

You may ask to view, correct or delete your information, ask us to stop processing it, and withdraw your consent to the AI feature at any time. You can edit and delete on-device information in the app, and turn off AI in the app's AI settings. For anything else, please use the email in section 1.

10. About the AI features

  • A category chosen by AI is shown with an "AI suggestion" label and a reason. You can change it before saving; an entry is saved only after you check it and save.
  • On the confirmation screen for an AI-chosen category, "Report AI result" opens your phone's email composer. The email body contains only the category the AI chose and the reason sentence the AI wrote — not the amount, the merchant or the line you typed. You can read and edit it before sending; it is addressed to ryussuny@gmail.com.
  • Weekly coach reports show an "AI-generated" label and the sentence "AI-generated estimate from your own records. Not financial advice." The amounts on screen are numbers calculated by the server; text written by the AI is not shown as is.
  • In this free version there is a monthly limit on automatic categorizing and on weekly coach reports.
