3. What is sent to the server

Without the consent marker the app does not even create the request and shows only the hints found on the phone. The coach is the same: it opens the consent screen first, and if you say "Not now" it sends nothing. If you do not agree, the on-device hints and the calculator still work, and the coach stays off.

  • The application coach sends more than the scam check. It sends your background notes, the job posting and (optionally) the writing sample, as listed above — not only a pasted posting. The coach works in English only; text with Hangul is refused on the phone before sending. The server allows 1 draft a month on the free plan.
  • The app hides long numbers before sending. In the posting (scam check), in the coach's notes, posting and writing sample, and in the note and your own text of a report, a number of 8 or more digits is replaced with # (same length) before it leaves the phone. Digits count together when they are written together or split by single spaces (also tabs, non-breaking spaces and up to 3 spaces in a row), hyphens, dots or slashes (a closing bracket ) may be followed by a space) — so a national ID, card, account or phone number written in one of those forms is hidden; fullwidth digits count too. Kept as written: numbers of fewer than 8 digits; amounts with commas or decimal points; year ranges and lists (2019-2023, 2019 2021 2023); dates (2023-05-01, 05/01/2023, 12.12.2012); amounts with dot thousands (12.500.000); lists of 1–2 digit groups with 8–9 digits (10 20 30 40). Not recognised (limits): numbers split by commas, by - , by line breaks or by 4 or more spaces; eight digits written together as a date (20230501) or an amount without commas (50000000) are hidden like an account number; names, addresses, email addresses and numbers shorter than 8 digits are not hidden; the AI answer quoted in a report is not changed. The keyword and link hints shown on the phone use the text as you wrote it, and risky links the phone finds in the original text are added to the server's result. A # can therefore appear in a coach draft; the draft screen then shows a one-line note to fill in the real number.
  • The server does not store what you send or the AI's answer. Server logs keep only processing details such as the account ID, function, token counts, cost and status — never your text. It does not keep the coach's notes, posting, writing sample or draft either.

7. Keeping and deleting; how to ask for deletion

Information on your device

Nothing you type or paste is kept on the device, so there is nothing to retain or delete — except one small marker that you agreed to the AI features, which "Delete account and data" in the app (or clearing the app's data) erases. What you type or paste stays only in the phone's memory while the app is open; closing the app removes it.

Information on the server

You can delete your account from inside the app: open the menu (three dots) at the top of the screen and choose "Delete account and data". The screen asks you to confirm twice. It deletes your account on our server and your sign-in account (or uses a server fallback to delete it), signs you out, and then erases the AI consent marker on the phone — the phone keeps nothing else. It does not create a new account afterwards. The screen tells you if you are offline or the server returns an error, and if a step fails after the data on our server was already deleted it says so ("your data on our server is already deleted ... try again and it will finish").

On deletion, the whole account document is removed; reports you sent lose their link to your account and the text you wrote (the response as shown, your posting, your note); the duplicate-request records are removed; and a re-registration block (a hash of the account ID) stays for 30 days and is then removed automatically.

Alternatively, to have your server information deleted, please email ryussuny@gmail.com, or see the account and data deletion page. If you include roughly when you first used the app and whether you used the AI features, we will delete what we can identify within 10 days and tell you the result. Because the app does not show you an account identifier, we will tell you if we cannot identify your account.

8. Your rights

You may ask to view, correct or delete your information, ask us to stop processing it, and withdraw your consent to the AI features at any time. You can turn the AI features off (withdraw your consent) in the app; if the withdrawal could not be recorded on the server, the app sends it once more when it returns to the foreground. You can delete your account and data in the app, as described in section 7. For anything else, please use the email in section 1.

  • The consent screen (one screen, opened from the scam check, from the coach and from the AI switch) says, in short: both features use an AI model run by Anthropic; the scam check sends the posting you paste and the coach sends your background notes, the job posting and the writing sample if you add one; "Before anything is sent, numbers of 8 or more digits (for example account, card or phone numbers, written together or split by spaces, hyphens, dots or slashes) are hidden with #. Years, dates and amounts with commas stay as written. Numbers split by commas or line breaks may not be recognized, and names, addresses and email addresses are not hidden, so please don't enter them."; "Our server doesn't keep what you send or the AI's answer. If you report a result, the result is kept for review. Your own text (the posting, or for a draft your notes and the posting) is kept only if you tick that box."; transfer abroad to Anthropic, PBC in the United States each time you check a posting or ask for a draft; if you don't agree nothing is sent, the on-device hints and the calculator still work and the coach stays off.
  • When the server returns an AI-generated result the app shows an AI-generated label. In the scam check the AI does not write sentences: it returns signal codes and excerpts from the posting, and every sentence you read is fixed app text. The app rejects a result that lacks the "not definitive" marker. If the server says "no problems" while warning signs are present, the app discards it and shows "could not check". When there are no hints and no AI result, the app does not say "no problems"; it says the check could not be done.
  • Application coach: every draft screen carries the "AI-generated" label whatever the server flag says, the sentence "AI-generated draft. Check every detail against your own experience before you send it.", the app's own greeting and closing (not model text), and for every sentence the note of yours it comes from. A reply whose draft does not match its quoted notes is not shown. The sentences the server removed are counted on screen.
  • Reporting AI output: the scam check shows a report button on every AI result; the coach shows one on every draft and on the "can't write" screen. A coach reply without report information is not shown at all. What a report contains and how long it is kept are in sections 3 and 4.

11. Children

This app is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 14.

12. Security measures

No input text is written to logs (a development rule that is checked automatically), app data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer, communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS), and the app uses anonymous sign-in and Firebase App Check.
